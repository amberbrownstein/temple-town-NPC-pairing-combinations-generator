Bring Back Temple Town! (working name) is a character-driven role-playing farming simulation game centered around a rural town struggling with an aging population. While the main underlying theme is about building community, other significant themes involve: familial expectations and forging your own path, globalization and cultural preservation, and nature and spirituality. Influences include Harvest Moon, Story of Seasons, and Stardew Valley, as well as the real-life struggles of rural towns, especially in Japan.

Like many farming games, this one will allow the player to date and marry certain townsfolk. However, each romanceable character (aside from some who are secret bachelors/bachelorettes) will have 2 different romantic rivals in the initial release of the game. While romantic rivals will not prevent a player from being able to date or marry their character of choice, it can add difficulty if said NPC develops a closer relationship with a rival than the one that they have with the player.

Once the player marries their partner of choice, the remaining romanceable characters will start developing deeper relationships with corresponding rivals and eventually get married themselves. As a result, each romanceable character has two assigned rivals so that each NPC will still be able to get married regardless of who the player chooses to marry.

While additional pairings may be supported in the future as downloadable content, this algorithm helped me determine the most ideal rival assignments to set as the default for the initial launch, based on which rivals I like best for each NPC and the limitation of each character needing to be assigned exactly two rivals. However, this continues to be useful, as my personal preferences regarding pairing rankings is always subject to change.
